Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

1. Who we are

BuyBay is an online shop in Bangladesh. This policy explains what personal information we handle and why.

2. Information we collect

  • Order details: name, mobile number, district, thana, full address, items ordered, payment method and bKash/Nagad Transaction ID.
  • Messages: anything you send us on Messenger, WhatsApp or by phone, including warranty claims and photos.
  • Browser storage: your cart and wishlist are saved in your own browser (local storage) so they are still there next visit. They never leave your device unless you place an order.

We do not run user accounts and do not collect card numbers, PINs or passwords.

3. How we use it

We use your information to confirm and deliver orders, verify payments, handle returns and warranty claims, and answer your questions. We do not sell your data.

4. Who we share it with

  • Couriers: your name, phone number and address, only to deliver your parcel.
  • Messaging services: when you press Place Order, your order is sent through Facebook Messenger or WhatsApp (Meta). Their own privacy policies apply to those chats.
  • Payment services: bKash and Nagad handle your payment on their own systems.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

5. Cookies and third parties

This site does not use advertising or analytics trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google may receive your IP address when a page loads.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order and warranty records only as long as needed for delivery, returns, warranty, accounting and legal reasons, then delete or anonymise them. Chats stay in Messenger or WhatsApp until deleted.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information, and you can clear your cart and wishlist at any time by clearing your browser data. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Security

We limit who can see order details and use reputable platforms to receive them. No online service is completely risk-free, so please never share your payment PIN or OTP with anyone, including us.

9. Children

Our site is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please shop with a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
