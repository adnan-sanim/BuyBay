Claim Warranty

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Buy with confidence. This page explains what our warranty covers and how to claim it.

Coverage

  • Electronics: 7-day replacement for a defective or dead-on-arrival item, plus a 6-month service warranty against manufacturing faults.
  • Fashion, home and sports items: 3-day replacement for damaged, wrong-size or wrong items.
  • Beauty and grocery: replaced or refunded only if damaged, leaking, or wrong on arrival. Please report within 24 hours.

Any different warranty period is written on the product page or told to you when your order is confirmed.

What is not covered

  • Physical damage, water damage, burns, or misuse.
  • Repairs or opening the product by anyone other than us or our service partner.
  • Normal wear, scratches, and used consumables.
  • Missing invoice or order ID, or a broken warranty seal where one was fitted.

How to claim

  1. Keep your order ID and the product with its box and accessories.
  2. Fill in the form below. Then send clear photos or a short video of the problem in the chat that opens.
  3. We reply within 24–48 hours to confirm if your claim is approved.
  4. Send the product to us or we arrange a courier pickup. Delivery cost for approved defects is on us.
  5. We repair or replace the item within 7–15 working days. If it cannot be fixed or replaced, you get a refund.
